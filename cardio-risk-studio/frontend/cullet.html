A FEW USEFUL THINGS

Before we begin.

How long does a commission take?

Most pieces take six to ten weeks from an agreed brief. Larger architectural work may need a longer lead time; we'll set a clear schedule before work begins.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes. Studio visits are available by appointment, and the half-day bench commission includes time in the hot shop with the maker.

Is every piece exactly the same?

No. Each piece is shaped by hand, so small shifts in color, thickness and form are part of the process. We share finish and scale references before making begins.

Where do you ship?

We ship insured within the United States and can arrange international delivery for larger commissions. Packing is designed for glass, not guesswork.
